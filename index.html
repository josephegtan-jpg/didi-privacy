DidITakeMyMed Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026
Applies to: DidITakeMyMed version 1.0 for Android
Developer: Joseph Tan, Petaling Jaya, Malaysia

In short

Your health information stays on your phone. Your medicines, doses, vitals, symptoms, conditions, profile and caregiver contact are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and we do not collect them.

The only thing that can leave your phone is sign-in information, and only if you choose to sign in. Signing in is optional. The app works fully without it. The app does not use your location, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting or ads.

1. Who we are

DidITakeMyMed is a medication reminder app for older adults and their families in Malaysia and Singapore. It is built and maintained by one independent developer, Joseph Tan, who is responsible for this app. Contact details are in section 13.

2. Health data stays on your device, and we do not collect it

Everything you enter into the app is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to any server, not synced to the cloud, and not visible to the developer. This includes:

  • Your profile: name, age, health conditions, and the symptoms you choose to track.
  • Caregiver contact details, if you enter them (name and phone number).
  • Your medicines: names, doses, schedules, course length and stock levels.
  • Dose history: which doses were taken, missed or snoozed, and when.
  • Vitals and symptoms: blood pressure, blood sugar and symptom records.
  • Appointments and reminder settings (such as check-in and going-out reminder days and times).
  • App settings: language, text size, notification preferences, and a record of when you accepted the Terms of Use.
  • A "last opened" time, which the app uses on your phone to decide whether to show you a gentle reminder to open it.

Because this data never leaves your phone, we have no copy of it and cannot see, share or sell it. It is protected by your phone's own security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. Anyone who can use your unlocked phone can open the app and see it, just as they could with any other app.

3. Optional sign-in

Signing in is optional. You can use every feature of the app without an account. If you choose to sign in, the app uses Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to create and manage your account. You can sign in in either of these ways:

  • Google Sign-In. Google confirms your identity and shares your Google account email address with the app.
  • Email and password. Firebase stores your email address and a securely hashed form of your password. Firebase sends account emails on our behalf, for example to verify your email address or reset your password.

When you sign in, Firebase Authentication keeps an account record containing: a randomly generated user ID, your email address, which sign-in method you used, whether your email is verified, and security information such as account creation and sign-in times and the IP address of sign-in requests. Google may use this information to secure the service, for example to detect abuse.

This account record does not contain any of your health information. Signing in does not upload your medicines, doses, vitals or profile. They stay on your phone either way. If you never sign in, no sign-in information leaves your phone at all.

4. What the app does not do

  • No location. The app does not request, use or collect your location, either in the foreground or in the background, and it does not use Google Maps.
  • No analytics or crash reporting. The app contains no analytics, usage-tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • No advertising. There are no ads, and the app does not read or share advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We have no advertising partners, analytics vendors or data brokers.
  • No health data on servers. We do not run any server or database that stores your health information.

5. Contacting your caregiver is always your choice

If you save a caregiver's phone number, the app can help you reach them. For example, you can say hello from the check-in reminder, or ask for help from a missed-dose alert. When you tap one of these buttons, the app opens your phone's own dialer, SMS app or WhatsApp with a suggested message filled in. Nothing is sent automatically. You see the message and decide whether to send it or make the call. That message then travels through your phone carrier or WhatsApp under their own terms, not through us.

6. Permissions the app uses

The app asks Android for the following permissions. None of them send any data to us. You can review or change them at any time in your phone's Settings, under Apps, then DidITakeMyMed.

  • Notifications: to show your medication reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so reminders go off at the exact time you set.
  • Ignore battery optimisation (optional): so your phone's battery saver does not silence or delay reminders. You can decline this.
  • Run at startup: so your reminders are restored after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration and keeping the device awake: so a reminder can vibrate and appear while the phone is asleep.

7. Backups and sharing are under your control

Backup. In Profile, then Backup, you can export your data as a single file. Your phone's share menu then lets you save or send it wherever you choose, such as your email or Google Drive. We never see this file. You can restore it later with Import.

Copy for Doctor. In Vitals, this button copies a plain-text summary of the readings you select to your phone's clipboard, so you can paste it into a message to your doctor. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

8. Third-party services

The only third-party service the app uses is sign-in, and only when you sign in:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication (Google LLC), as described in section 3. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Reminders use your phone's built-in notification system. Our reminders are scheduled on your phone and are not sent through any server of ours.

9. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it, delete your data in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Sign-in account (only if you signed in): kept while your account exists, and removed when you delete your account (see section 10).
  • With the developer: nothing. We do not receive your data, so we hold no copy of it.

10. How to delete your data

In the app

Go to Profile, then Advanced settings, then Delete. This permanently erases everything the app stores on your phone: your profile, medicines, schedules, history, vitals and settings. It also cancels all reminders. If you are signed in at the time, your sign-in account is deleted from Firebase Authentication as well. For security, Firebase may ask you to sign in again first.

Uninstalling the app also removes all data stored on your phone.

By contacting us

If you created an account and cannot delete it in the app, for example because you no longer have the app or were not signed in when you deleted your data, email josephegtan@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We will delete the sign-in account record and confirm by email. We cannot delete the data on your phone for you, because we never had access to it. Use the in-app Delete option or uninstall the app to remove it.

11. Children

DidITakeMyMed is designed for adults, particularly older adults managing their own medicines and the family members who help them. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created a sign-in account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights in Malaysia and Singapore

If you are in Malaysia, you have rights under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA 2010). If you are in Singapore, you have rights under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA 2012). These include the right to access and correct your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to ask for it to be deleted.

Because your health data stays on your phone, you can use most of these rights directly in the app:

  • Access: open the app, or export a backup file to see everything that is stored.
  • Correction: edit any entry, or update your profile in the app.
  • Withdrawing consent and deletion: use Delete in Profile, then Advanced settings, or uninstall the app. For a sign-in account, see section 10.

For requests about your sign-in account, or any complaint, contact us at the address below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP) in Malaysia (pdp.gov.my) or the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) in Singapore (pdpc.gov.sg).
